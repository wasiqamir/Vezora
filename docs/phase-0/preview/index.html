First release scope

A single person designs a logo, saves the editable project, reopens it, and exports SVG and PNG. A simple PNG or JPG can become a draft they correct by hand. Recovery from a photo is an approximation.

Who it is for

  • Startup founders who need a wordmark the same day
  • Freelancers customizing a template for a client
  • Small businesses that need a name, an icon, and a PNG
  • Branding teams starting a draft someone can reopen

Not in v1

  • Billing, teams, and sharing
  • Mobile canvas editing
  • PDF, EPS, or extra PNG sizes beyond 1× and 2×
  • Uploaded fonts and background removal
